# Borokini Couture — Digital Marketing & Operations Case Study

A real-world marketing and digital operations project completed for *Borokini Couture*, a bespoke fashion business.

This project demonstrates how I combined my background in *Marketing Management* with practical digital, content, and data-organization skills to strengthen a small business's online presence and create a foundation for future analysis.

---

## About the Project

Borokini Couture primarily relied on offline customer acquisition through walk-ins and referrals.

My role was to support the business in developing a stronger digital presence, improving how its garments were presented online, creating more consistent content and branding, and introducing a structured approach to customer-order information.

The project brought together:

- Digital marketing
- Social media management
- Content strategy
- Brand development
- Website development
- Pinterest management
- Visual content improvement
- Customer data organization

---

## Digital Marketing & Content

### Pinterest

I created the Pinterest account and supported content publishing to improve the brand's visual discoverability.

The account was developed from an initial setup into an active visual content channel, with performance monitored through Pinterest Analytics.

Key evidence includes:

- Before-and-after account development
- Pinterest analytics
- Top-performing pins
- Content performance observations

### Content Campaign

An *Outfit of the Week (OOTW)* campaign was used during the project period to showcase garments consistently and explore the types of fashion content that could appeal to the brand's intended audience.

The campaign provided practical experience in:

- Content planning
- Visual storytelling
- Audience-focused content
- Consistent publishing
- Performance observation

---

## Visual Content Improvement

One area of the project focused on improving how garments were presented.

I worked on transforming product imagery so that the garment became the main visual focus rather than being competing with distracting backgrounds or inconsistent presentation.

This created a more polished and consistent product-display style suitable for digital channels.

---

## Customer Data & Process Improvement

I created an *Excel-based customer-order template* to introduce a more structured way of recording customer and order information.

The template included fields such as:

- Customer information
- Garment/product details
- Order amount
- Deposit
- Outstanding balance
- Order status
- Delivery information

The purpose was to create a foundation that could eventually support reporting and analysis.

---

## Results & Evidence

The project produced several tangible deliverables:

- Business website
- Pinterest presence
- TikTok support
- Content strategy and publishing activity
- Branding recommendations
- Improved garment presentation
- Customer-order tracking structure

Pinterest also generated measurable platform activity during the period of active publishing.

The available analytics were treated as *platform-performance evidence rather than direct evidence of revenue or customer acquisition*, because reliable conversion attribution was not available.

---

## Limitations

The project had limited customer-order data available for analysis.

Because the dataset was too small to support reliable conclusions about customer behaviour, purchasing patterns, product demand or revenue trends, I did not present unsupported analytics findings.

Instead, the project focused on establishing the digital and data infrastructure required for meaningful analysis in the future.
